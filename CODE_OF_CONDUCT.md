# Contributor code of conduct

We want a respectful, practical community where contributors can ask questions,
report errors and disagree about engineering decisions without harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully regardless of experience, identity or background.
- Give specific technical feedback and assume good faith.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing and repeated personal attacks are unacceptable. These rules
apply in project issues, discussions, pull requests and other project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the maintainer through the contact options on
[Gowrav-M's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Gowrav-M). If no suitable private
channel is listed, ask for one without publishing personal or incident details.
Maintainers should protect reporters' privacy as far as reasonably possible.

Maintainers may request edits, remove harmful material, close discussions, issue
warnings, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently according to the
severity and repetition of behavior. Explain enforcement decisions privately when
appropriate. Good-faith technical disagreement is not a conduct violation.
